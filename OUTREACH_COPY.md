# Outreach copy

Use only where direct offers are allowed, or in a genuine one-to-one conversation. Replace every bracketed item with a real observation. Do not bulk-send.

For example, I would change [current element] to [specific recommendation]. I offer a fixed $100 Lead Leak Fix Pack: a prioritized teardown, replacement hero/CTA copy, FAQ content, and an implementation order, delivered within 24 hours. Quick ask: I launched a one-day website conversion fix pack for service businesses. It is $100 flat and includes actual replacement copy—not an automated score. If you know one owner whose site gets visits but not enough calls or quote requests, would you introduce us? Sample: [public sample URL].

## Qualification checklist

- They publicly asked for website, conversion, copy, lead, booking, or quote-path help.
- Their site is public and has a clear commercial conversion action.
- You can identify one concrete issue without private data.
- The channel permits offers or direct contact.
- The message includes no invented results, credentials, scarcity, or guarantees.

## Stop rules

- Do not contact people who did not invite offers in communities that ban promotion.
- Do not scrape or purchase email lists.
- Do not send more than one follow-up.
- Do not promise rankings, lead volume, revenue, or a conversion-rate lift.
