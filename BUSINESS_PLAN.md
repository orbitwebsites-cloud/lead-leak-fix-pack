# Lead Leak Fix Pack — 10-Hour Launch Plan

## Target

One legitimate $100 sale of a same-day website conversion implementation pack. Revenue is a target, not a guarantee; it depends on a buyer choosing to purchase.

## Offer

- Buyer: owner of a local service business, consultant, agency, or B2B service with a public website.
- Trigger: the site gets visits but generates too few calls, quote requests, bookings, or demos.
- Deliverable: 8–12 prioritized findings, annotated evidence, replacement hero/CTA copy, at least five FAQ answers, schema-ready content, and an implementation order.
- Price: $100 once, paid before work begins.
- Delivery: within 24 hours after receiving the URL and contact email.
- Boundaries: one public site; no credentials required; no guarantee of revenue; not a full redesign or technical SEO crawl.

## Brutal startup test

| Test | Kill threshold | Evidence | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pain | Buyer cannot connect site friction to missed leads | Service sites live or die on calls, quotes, bookings, and demos | Pass |
| Price | Comparable fixed audits consistently cost below $50 | Current offers range from low-end marketplace gigs to $79–$250+ specialists | Pass at $100 only with usable copy/fixes |
| Fulfillment | One order takes more than four hours | Tight scope and reusable report structure make 2–3 hours plausible | Pass |
| Proof | Cannot demonstrate quality without a client | Clearly labeled fictional sample shows the exact output | Pass |
| Payment | Checkout requires manual invoicing | Stripe Payment Link supports immediate card payment | Pass |
| Reach | Fewer than 20 qualified, reachable buyers with current intent | General cold outreach and promotion-hostile forums do not satisfy this reliably | **Fail / dominant risk** |
| Trust | Offer depends on fake results or credentials | Honest scope, sample, secure checkout, and no invented testimonials | Pass |

## Why the original $19 template idea was killed

A low-ticket download requires meaningful existing traffic. At a typical low single-digit conversion rate, five or six purchases would need hundreds of qualified visitors—implausible from zero in ten hours without ad spend or an audience.

## Surviving distribution strategy

1. Prioritize buyers already asking for website or conversion help on marketplaces, job boards, founder communities, or an existing personal network.
2. Send a short observation about their actual site, not a bulk template.
3. Give one useful issue free, link the sample, and offer the fixed $100 pack.
4. Do not spam communities that ban promotion. Do not claim past results that do not exist.
5. Stop after 30 high-quality contacts and reassess; mass sending lowers trust and creates compliance risk.

## Funnel math

- Goal: 1 sale.
- At a 5% close rate from a qualified conversation: 20 conversations.
- At a 20% reply rate from highly personalized outreach: 100 messages would be needed for 20 conversations.
- In 8–10 hours, 100 genuinely personalized messages are not realistic. The viable path is 10–30 warm or high-intent contacts, where intent—not volume—raises the close probability.

## 10-hour operating schedule

1. Hour 0–2: finish proof, checkout, intake, and public page.
2. Hour 2–4: identify 20–30 high-intent prospects and record one real leak per site.
3. Hour 4–7: send individualized, rules-compliant offers through accounts the owner is authorized to use.
4. Hour 7–9: answer interested buyers quickly and share checkout.
5. Hour 9–10: begin fulfillment immediately if purchased; otherwise preserve the pipeline and revise the channel, not the core deliverable.

## Non-negotiable reality check

No ethical operator can guarantee a stranger will buy within ten hours. The assets can be completed and the offer can be launched; buyer behavior cannot be forced. A zero-sale night would falsify the distribution assumption, not prove the product has no value.
